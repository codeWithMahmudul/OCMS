Features

Designed for organizations & students

Simple, modern, and focused on anonymity and tenant isolation — a practical demo for university projects and small organizations.

Anonymous Submission

Submit complaints without sharing identity. A tracking ID is returned for follow up.

Multi-tenant

Tenant separation demonstrated via separate org portals and data isolation (concept).

Secure by design

Passwords hashed and careful handling of anonymous submissions (backend to implement).

Complaint Workflow

Track complaints, assign to officers, and manage statuses.

Notifications (future)

Placeholder for email/notification features to be added later.

Responsive UI

Designed mobile-first with Bootstrap so it works on phones and tablets.

Process

Submit a complaint in 4 simple steps

Clear process for anonymous and registered users.

01
Register

Create an account (optional for anonymous) and pick your organization.

02
Submit

Fill complaint details, select category, and attach evidence if any.

03
Investigate

Assigned officers review and update status. Anonymous flag respected.

04
Resolve

Resolution notes posted and tracking ID used to follow progress.
